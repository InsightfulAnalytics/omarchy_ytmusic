Everything the panel does, area by area. The [README](../README.md) has the overview.

| Where | Action |
|---|---|
| Bar icon | Left click: open/close the panel · Middle click: play/pause · Right click: open/close the panel · Wheel: previous/next track |
| Shortcut | Open/close the panel, once bound (`{ panel = "tim.ytmusic" }` in `~/.config/hypr/bindings.lua`; see the README) |
| Panel tabs | **Now** (playing + queue, like, dislike (the song skips), add to playlist, **Save** the queue into a playlist, Resume; an artist, the album or the cover opens its page; the queue header's pane chip, or `y`, switches the area below to **Lyrics**, time-synced with the line being sung highlighted when YouTube has them, or **Related**, the web's Related tab as a page), **Search** (field, filter chips with More, a scope chip, grouped results with Show all), **Library** (account (`M`: Add artists you like, Your channel, Switch account, Audio quality, Content location, Record plays in history, Play with your account, Sign out), new playlist (with a privacy chip, `Alt+Left` / `Alt+Right` while typing, and an optional description; an invite link pasted there joins that playlist instead), and chips switched with `h` / `l`: **Playlists** with Liked Music, History, Recent activity and your and saved playlists; **Songs** with Shuffle all (YouTube's shuffle of the whole library, which goes on through it past the first 50 songs; Enter on a song plays from there through the rest of the library in its order); **Albums**; **Artists** with Subscriptions and Profiles; **Podcasts** with Episodes for Later (and how many), New episodes and Channels, then your saved podcasts; **Uploads** with Upload a file (a path field: `Enter` uploads, `Esc` leaves; YouTube lists the song once it has processed it), your uploaded Albums and Artists (each a page), then your uploaded songs, which play with your account (a row's menu deletes a song or an album, after a second press). `O` or the sort button sends the chip's next sort order, which YouTube saves on the account in use as the web does; the first heading names the order YouTube says the list is in. The chip is remembered, the order is not: every other read (opening the chip, the playlist picker, a refresh, another account) sends none, so it never changes a sort you set on the web, your phone or another identity. Long lists load about 50 at a time, to their end (up to 10,000 items; past that the list says so and `O` shows the rest from the other end)), **Home** (every shelf of the web's feed, loaded a few at a time as you scroll, with the mood chips above it switched with `h` / `l`; a shelf ends in **See all** when the web has one and in **Play all** when it plays as a list, the list YouTube makes for it; a song plays its shelf from there, a mix opens as its page, and a podcast as its list of episodes) and **Explore** (`5`: rows for **New releases**, **Charts** and **Moods & genres**, then New albums & singles, Top songs (Premium accounts), Moods & genres (eight, and a row that shows all of them: some are on no other page), Trending, New music videos and Popular episodes when YouTube has them, each ending in See all; chart songs and artists lead with their rank and a rising, falling or steady mark. **Charts** opens in the country you chose last (Global at first): its first row, `O` or its `M` menu lists YouTube's countries, and the choice is remembered. The Moods & genres page lists its groups (For you, Moods & moments, Genres), and a category its playlists in the web's titled shelves. When the five tab labels do not fit, the tabs show icons); albums, playlists, mixes and artists open as pages with Play / Shuffle / Queue all (a page that shows part of its list, such as Liked Music past 200 songs or an artist's five top songs, plays all of it). An album or playlist header has its byline (album type, artists and year, or the playlist's author; names are links, and `A` with no row selected opens the album's artist or the playlist's author; a playlist's `M` has **Go to author**), details (privacy of your own playlist, songs, duration, views, year, and how many songs are unavailable) and two lines of the description, with an **About** row for the rest; songs marked explicit carry a small boxed E. Liked Music has YouTube's mood and genre chips (Party, Chill, Rock and the rest, made from your likes): **All**, the first three and **More** (a chooser of every one), cycled with `h` / `l` and in its `M` menu (**Filter**); a chip reads the list again filtered, and Play then plays what it shows. A playlist or Liked Music longer than its first read (200 to 300 songs) ends in **Load all N songs**, which reloads the whole list (up to 5000) with the cursor kept; `f` filters the page's songs by title, artist or album, ignoring case and accents (it loads the whole list first; Down goes to the matches, Esc clears it). An album lists its track numbers and then Other versions and the served recommendations, a playlist its related playlists, and a mix (My Supermix and the like) its first 50 songs; your own playlists can remove songs and be deleted (a second click within 4 s confirms), be edited (`e` or **Edit**: title, description and privacy and, under Advanced, the order and voting (the choices YouTube offers, under its own names), where new songs go, an invite link for collaborators that is copied (it needs wl-copy), and Close collaboration while it is on; **Remove custom image** when YouTube offers it, chosen twice within 4 s; what YouTube does not report says so, and YouTube cannot clear a description), have songs moved with `J` / `K` or a row's arrows (a playlist sorted by date or votes asks for Manual order first), and end in YouTube's **Suggestions** (`i` adds the one under the cursor, Enter plays it, **Refresh suggestions** asks again); songs show their net votes (`+3`) when voting is on. Sectioned pages end each section in a selectable **See all** row and long lists in **Load more**, which also loads by itself when the cursor nears the end, to the end of the list (a page that reaches 10,000 items says so). An artist's page has every shelf of the web page in its order, from Top songs to Fans might also like (and From your library when signed in); See all opens the whole list (all albums or singles, with the served sort orders as chips), and the header adds **Radio** (the artist's radio mix) and **About** (description and links; Enter on a link copies it), while **Shuffle** plays YouTube's shuffle mix of the artist. An album, a playlist that is not yours and a mix have **Save** (**Saved** once in your library) and an artist **Subscribe** (**Subscribed**); `S` saves or subscribes the row under the cursor (a song too, from a list that offers it) or, with no row, the page, and `D` dislikes the row's song or the one playing, which skips. The change shows at once and is put back if YouTube refuses it; if the worker stopped before answering, the page or Library list is read again and a notice says so (on Search, Home and Explore it asks you to open the item again to check) |
| Menus | Every row has a menu (`m`, `.`, right-click or its ⋮ button), like the web's three-dot menu: Play, Shuffle, Play next, Add to queue, Start radio, Save to library or Remove from library (albums, playlists, mixes, songs, the song playing and every queue row; a song in search results read while typing first has its own menu read; never an upload, as on the web), Subscribe or Unsubscribe, Add to or Remove from Liked Music, Dislike or Remove dislike, Add to playlist, Go to artist (a chooser when a song has several) or album, View credits (a song's performers, writers and producers as a page), Copy link (a music.youtube.com link, through `wl-copy`), Pin to or Unpin from Listen again (songs, videos, albums, playlists, artists, podcasts and episodes on any list that serves it (in search results, songs only; a song in results read while typing has it read from its own menu), Listen again's See all page, the song playing and every queue row, never an upload; the page menu pins an album, playlist, Liked Music, artist, podcast or episode page; YouTube sometimes keeps an unpinned item there for a while), Not interested and Don't recommend artist on Home's cards (choose it twice within 4 s; the card leaves Home at once, and as with the web's Removed from Home toast, Home's `M` menu then offers Undo for 2 minutes), Save to playlist (a whole album, playlist or mix, a mix by the 50 songs it plays; `p` on its row too) and Copy to new playlist (the picker asks again when a song is already there: Enter again adds it anyway, and when several songs or a whole album or playlist are refused, the **Skip duplicates** row under that playlist adds only the songs not there yet), Remove, Remove from <playlist> (a queue row or the song playing, while the queue plays one of your own playlists; the queue keeps the song), Remove from history (History rows; choose it twice), and for the song playing Play video version or Play song version. `n` / `a` queue a whole album, playlist or mix from any list (a mix's menu also has Shuffle), and `R` starts radio from the row, else from the artist page or Liked Music you are on, else from the song playing (Liked Music's row and page menus have **Start radio** too: YouTube's radio of your likes); radio replaces the queue, as on the web (from the song playing, that song plays on). A list longer than 500 songs (a playlist, Liked Music, a big album, your library's songs or uploads) goes into the queue 500 at a time and plays to its end, as on the web: once 3 or fewer songs are left the next ones follow, before autoplay and whether or not autoplay is on, and the queue's last line says what follows. A song played on its own keeps it going, and so does Resume after mpv's idle quit while the shell keeps running (the rest of the list is held in memory only: after a logout, reboot or shell restart the list ends where the restored queue does); a new list, Clear, Stop and an emptied queue end it. Add to queue of a long list goes on the same way at the end of the queue, unless a list already goes on there; Play next of one queues its first 500, with a notice. Repeat all keeps the whole list in the queue once it has come round. A list over 5000 songs plays its first 5000 and says so. `M` opens the page's own menu: the header buttons' twins on a page (an artist's adds About; an album or playlist adds Load all, Find and About; your own playlist Edit playlist), the account on Library (**Add artists you like**: YouTube's suggested artists as a page, where `f` finds, Enter marks or unmarks one and the **Add N artists** row, pressed twice, adds the marked ones to your taste profile; it can only add, removing one is done on the web. Then Your channel, Switch account, Audio quality, Content location, Record plays in history, Play with your account and Sign out), Autoplay, Clear, Save the queue, Tune the queue, Playback speed and Loudness on Now, the scopes and More filters on Search, Refresh (and that Undo) on Home, Refresh on Explore, the country on Charts. `?` lists every key; Enter on one runs it. |
| Resume | After mpv has quit (idle timeout, logout, reboot) play/pause (middle click, media keys, `omarchy-shell ytmusic resume` or the Resume button) restores the queue, the track and its position. The snapshot is `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/session.json`, saved after queue changes, on pause and every 30 s while playing. |
| Search | Results appear as you type, read signed out (as on the web, words you have not submitted never reach your account's YouTube search history); Enter, or a picked suggestion or recent search, searches once with your account, which YouTube records. A **Library** or **Uploads** search runs on Enter only, and the status says so; choosing that scope or a filter for words you have not submitted waits for Enter too (words already submitted are searched in it at once). A song in the results read while typing gets Save to library and Pin to Listen again from its own menu, read when you press `m` or `S` on it (the menu opens once it has answered; `S` says it is loading, then works), never from a search with your account. **All** lists every kind of result in the order YouTube serves them under the top result (an artist's top result plays its Shuffle and Radio mixes from the menu): songs, videos, albums, artists, playlists, podcasts, episodes and profiles, each ending in **Show all**, which switches to that filter. The chips are All, Songs, Albums, Artists, Playlists and **More** (Videos, Featured and Community playlists, Podcasts, Episodes, Profiles; the chip then shows the one chosen); `h` / `l` cycle through all of them. A filtered list loads more as the cursor nears its end, until YouTube has no more (up to 2000 results; past that the list says so). Signed in, the chip at the end of the status line (or `M`) searches **YouTube Music**, your **Library** or your **Uploads**. When YouTube corrected your spelling the status says "Showing results for ..." and a **Search instead for** row searches the words as typed. An empty field lists your recent searches merged with your YouTube searches (signed in); `x` removes one, from YouTube too. While typing, up to two matching recent searches and every suggestion YouTube Music serves show above the results, then up to five artists, songs or albums that open or play; click one, or `Tab` / `Shift+Tab` to put a suggestion in the field. A search is remembered only when it led somewhere (Enter, a result played, opened or queued, or an entry chosen from a result's menu), in `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/searches.json`. Profiles, podcasts and episodes open as their own pages; uploaded albums and artists open as their upload pages. |
| Podcasts | A podcast opens as its page: its channel (a link), **Save** (`S`), and its episodes, newest first, loaded as you scroll; Enter plays the list from that episode. An episode's row shows its date, length, podcast and how much YouTube says you played; its menu has **Go to episode** and **Go to podcast** (`o`), and **Save to Episodes for Later** (`S`). An episode's page has **Play**, or **Resume** and **Play from start** once it was started (while that episode is the one playing, **Play** / **Pause** and **Play from start** act on it in place, the queue kept), **Save** (Episodes for Later), its description (About) and its **Chapters**: Enter on a chapter jumps there when that episode plays, otherwise it plays the episode from there. A started episode goes on from where you left it in this panel (kept every 15 s while it plays and on pause, skip, stop or another song started, for the last 200 episodes, in `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/positions.json`), else from where YouTube says you were (the length minus its "2 hr 16 min left", else its percentage through mpv's `start=<pct>%`); a finished one starts over. Your place is not sent back to YouTube. A podcast channel's page has its Latest episodes (See all lists every one), podcasts, videos and playlists, and **Subscribe**; the library's Channels are the ones you subscribe to (`S` unsubscribes). Episodes for Later removes an episode with `x` or `S`; New episodes lists the latest from the podcasts you saved. Episodes keep their own playback speed. A podcast's episodes come in the orders and filters YouTube serves, as on the web: the sort chip (a click opens Latest, Oldest, Popular, Creator provided; `O` cycles them) and the filter chips In progress, Unplayed and Played (a click picks one, a second click shows all again; `h` / `l` cycle them), both also in its `M` menu (**Sort**, **Filter**); a filter nothing matches says so, with a **Show all episodes** row. `f` (**Find in show**) loads every episode of the show, about 100 at a time, and filters them by title and the start of their description; a show longer than the panel keeps (10,000 episodes) is searched up to there, and the rows say so. An artist's Latest episodes show each episode's date and length, and See all lists the channel's episodes; a podcast channel's header shows its round avatar. An artist YouTube serves with a header and nothing under it (some authors) opens as that header, with Subscribe and a note. |
| Profiles | A profile (a user channel: from Search, Home, Recent activity, Subscriptions or a playlist's author) opens as its page: its rows as the web shows them (Videos, Playlists and the like), each ending in **See all** when the web has one, and **Subscribe** / **Subscribed** (`S` or `M`; the change shows at once and is put back if YouTube refuses it). A profile row has a round cover and a menu: Open, Subscribe or Unsubscribe, Copy link. **Your channel** (Library's `M` menu) is your own channel's page, led by your mix and with your public playlists, without Subscribe; when YouTube's account menu links no channel, the page says so and the entry goes. The Artists chip's **Profiles** row lists the profiles you subscribe to (`S` unsubscribes), with the note YouTube serves under them ("Only profiles with music activity are shown.") and its sort orders as chips (Recently saved, A to Z, Z to A; `O` cycles them, and YouTube saves the order on your account as the web does). Your channel's pages are never kept in the disk cache: they carry your account's name. |
| Shuffle / repeat | Buttons around the transport (or `s` / `r`). Shuffle mixes the songs after the current one and turning it off puts them back in their order (songs added meanwhile keep their places); with shuffle on, a clicked song plays first and the rest of its album or playlist follows shuffled, and a page's **Shuffle** button turns it on (an artist's plays YouTube's shuffle mix instead). Repeat cycles off → queue → this song (mpv's `loop-playlist` / `loop-file`, so MPRIS sees it too). Both are remembered in `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/player.json`. |
| Autoplay | On by default (**Autoplay** in the queue header, IPC `autoplay`): like the web's Up next, the queue never runs dry. Five seconds into a song with 3 or fewer songs after it, up to 10 more are appended as radio for whatever plays: an album or playlist goes on as its radio, a radio as it is, a single song as its own mix (songs before videos, nothing this queue already holds or played). If every pick is a repeat it tries the song's own radio once, then says "Autoplay found nothing new". The queue header shows **Playing from** (the album, playlist or radio) and one **Tune** chip (`t`, or a click, cycles All, Discover, Popular, Deep cuts and the rest; the Now page menu `M` lists them all): a chip replaces only the songs autoplay added, never yours, and songs you add go before autoplay's. The songs autoplay added that have not played yet sit under an **Autoplay** caption, dimmed, as on the web (a radio, or a queue tuned to a chip other than All, is plain rows, as there); songs from Start radio, autoplay, a Tune chip and Library's Shuffle all carry their explicit E like any other. For the song playing, its menu offers **Play video version** or **Play song version** when YouTube has the other one. Played songs more than 50 behind the current one leave the queue, so it stays small however long it runs; which entries autoplay added is kept in `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/queue.json`. Off while repeat is on. The rest of a long list (more than 500 songs) comes before autoplay, as your own songs rather than autoplay's, also with autoplay off or repeat all on. |
| Speed / loudness | `[` / `]` (any tab) or **Playback speed** in the Now page menu (`M`) play at 0.5x to 2x in steps of 0.25; Now shows a small **1.5x** badge (click it for the speeds) while it is not 1x. Music and podcast episodes keep separate speeds, applied whenever the kind of entry playing changes. **Loudness** in the same menu: **Off** (as the music comes), **YouTube** (like the web player: a song YouTube measures louder than its target is turned down by that much, never boosted; one more request per resolved song) or **Dynamic** (mpv's `dynaudnorm`, which evens out loud and quiet passages and so changes the music's dynamics). Both are remembered in `player.json`. A player started before `mpv/ytm-levels.lua` existed has it loaded by the shell (mpv's `load-script`). |
| Play with your account | Off until you turn it on: **Play with your account** in Library's account `M` menu. Songs are then looked up with your account's cookies by the worker (mpv never gets them), instead of signed out, while you use your own account as the browser's first Google login (a brand account or another login looks songs up signed out). YouTube's bot check ("Sign in to confirm you're not a bot") rarely refuses a signed-in lookup, and with Premium yt-dlp needs no proof tokens. The cost: YouTube sees your account, not only your address, fetching the streams, and yt-dlp's docs warn that an account used this way can be flagged. Signed out, or turned off, lookups are anonymous again. |
| History | Off until you turn it on: **Record plays** on Library's account row (it reads **Recording plays** while on), **Record plays in history** in its `M` menu, or IPC `recordPlays`. Then every song, video or episode played for 10 s or more (mpv's position; a repeat-one loop counts again) goes to your YouTube Music history, as on the web, so it reaches History, Listen again, Recaps and your recommendations. Plays wait in `~/.cache/omarchy-ytmusic/pending-plays.json` (at most 200, none older than a day) and go in one batch when the worker is running anyway, when a panel opens, when 5 are waiting or 15 minutes after the oldest, so playing with the panel closed never starts the worker for one song. A batch whose outcome is unknown (the worker or the shell stopped mid-call) is dropped, not sent again: a missing play beats one counted twice. Turning it off, signing out or switching account drops what waits. While it is off, the first play of 10 s shows a one-time notice saying how to turn it on. On the History page, `x` pressed twice within 4 s (or the row's remove button clicked twice, or **Remove from history** in its menu) removes the entry from your YouTube Music history; it goes at once and comes back if YouTube refuses. |
| Account / quality / location | Library's account row shows the account in use (its photo or name, or `M`, opens its menu). **Switch account** lists the identities of your session as the web's account switcher does: your own account and its brand accounts (and other Google accounts the browser is signed in to, when YouTube lists them); choosing one makes every later read and edit (Library, likes, playlists, history) go to it until you switch back, the row shows its name with **Brand account** under it, and the panel reads everything again (**Your channel** then opens that account's own channel). The choice is kept in `~/.config/omarchy-ytmusic/identity.json` (ids only, no names), so `bin/ytm` uses it too; signing in or out resets it. Another Google account is reached by signing in again as it (`bin/ytm auth --authuser N`, N its place in Chromium's account list). A brand account of another Google account signs in again as that account first, then you switch to it. If YouTube stops answering as the brand account in use (no longer managed), the session reads as expired and Library offers **Use your own account** besides signing in again. A brand account cannot remove History entries (YouTube Music does not support it): the remove button and menu entry are gone there and `x` says why. **Audio quality**: **Low** (about 70 kbps), **Normal** (about 128 kbps) or **High** (the best stream, Opus at about 130 to 160 kbps; the default), from the next song on, for the worker's stream lookups and mpv's own fallback alike. **Content location**: **Default** (YouTube decides from your connection) or one of the 109 countries YouTube Music supports, sent with every request for Home, Explore and search (signed in, the account's own region still decides most of Top songs and Trending), and Charts then opens in that country when Charts has it (68 countries do; for the others Charts stays where it was); the language stays English. Both are remembered in `player.json`. |

### Signing in

`bin/ytm auth` (or **Sign in** in the Library tab) copies the YouTube session
from your Chromium profile (`~/.config/chromium/Default`; another one with
`--from-browser chromium:PROFILE`) straight from the cookie store, never through the
clipboard (Omarchy's clipboard history is saved in plain text). The new
session is verified before it replaces the old one. From the session
directory the shell reads only `account.json` (name, handle, photo) and
`identity.json` (ids only); if YouTube stops accepting the session you get one
notification (click it to sign in again) and a banner in the panel, while
search and playback keep working. `bin/ytm auth --paste` accepts request
headers instead.

### Not in the panel

These stay on music.youtube.com, by design:

- **Report** (a song or video): YouTube's moderation form, which only a
  browser can show and send, and ytmusicapi has no method for. Copy link
  (in a song's menu; an upload has none) gives the URL to report it from.
- **Setting a custom playlist image**: it uploads an image through
  YouTube's uploader, which needs a file chooser and an upload ytmusicapi
  has no method for. Removing one is in the playlist editor (when YouTube
  offers it).

The bar shows only the music icon (the song is in its tooltip). The panel is created on first open and unloaded 45 s after it
closes, so it holds no memory while you're not looking at it.
